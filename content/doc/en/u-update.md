# Update the GoalFinder Device
On this page, you can find the steps required to update your GoalFinder device.

## 1. Download the Update
Download the latest update from our [downloads page](https://goalfinder.github.io/download). You should see a `system.gfpkg` file in your downloads folder.

## 2. Upload the Update
Upload the downloaded update to the device by navigating to the system settings and clicking `Upload Firmware`.
In the software update dialog, click `Upload File`, select the `system.gfpkg` file you just downloaded, and then click `Upload Update`.
#-h Never Interrupt an Update
#-b Interrupting an update can corrupt the device storage and cause the device to stop functioning properly.
#-b Keep the device plugged in throughout the entire update process and do not perform any other actions through the web app.
