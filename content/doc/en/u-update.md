# Update the GoalFinder device
On this page you can find the steps necessary to update your GoalFinder device.

## 1. Download the update
Download the newest update from our [downloads page](https://goalfinder.github.io/download). You should see a `system.gfpkg` file in your downloads.

## 2. Upload the update
Upload the downloaded update to the device by navigating to the system settings and clicking on `Upload Firmware`.
In the software update dialog click upload file and select the `system.gfpkg` file you just downloaded and click `Upload Update`
#-h Never interrupt an update
#-b Interrupted updates can lead to corruption of the device storage and lead to the device not working properly.
#-b Keep the device plugged in during the entire update process and don't trigger any other actions through the web page.

#end
