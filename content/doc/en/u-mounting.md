# Mounting the GoalFinder Device
#wh Important Notice
#wb For the device to function as intended, it **must always** have a clear line of sight from the cutout for the distance sensor (more information on the location of the distance sensor can be found ^[here](../content/doc/en/u-getting-to-know.md)) through the entire basket.
Mounting the GoalFinder device on a basket can be done in two different ways:

## Mounting Variant
As mentioned in the ^[build guide](../content/doc/en/bg-assembly.md), the GoalFinder device can be assembled in a mounting variant, as shown below:
![Mounting Variant](assets/img/design/cad/3d-mounting.png)(600)
With the mounting variant, the device can be screwed onto the basket.

## Non-Mounting Variant
If using the mounting variant is not possible, the device in its base variant can also be attached using duct tape, although this may result in a less optimal experience due to reduced measurement accuracy.
