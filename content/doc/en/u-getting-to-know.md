# Getting to know the GoalFinder
## What it is supposed to do
The GoalFinder device aims to make basketball more accessible for blind and visually impaired players. By helping users locate the basket and providing immediate feedback on hits and misses, it enables them to participate in the game more independently and enjoy an experience similar to that of every other player.

## How it does that
The GoalFinder device uses a collection of input and output components to achieve it's functionality.
### Input
A **shake sensor** to detect the ball hitting the basket.
![Shake Sensor](assets/img/assembly/general/P1440892-N.JPG)(623x450)

A **distance sensor** to detect the ball crossing through the ring to detect hits.
![Distance Sensor](assets/img/showcase/P1440908-N.JPG)(403x450)

### Output
A LED Strip and Speaker to provide audio-visual feedback on hits and misses.
![LED Strip](assets\img\mounting\P1440944.JPG)(650)

The GoalFinder device also features a web app for setting up and controlling the device. Read more about it ^[here](../content/doc/en/u-webapp.md).
