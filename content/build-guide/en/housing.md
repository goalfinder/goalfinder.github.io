## Inner Mounts

The inner mounts are 3 mounts for PCB, sensors and accessories.

## Outer Shell

The outer part of the housing consists of 6 plates of which two can be switched out with a mounting version depending on mounting needs, which in the example are cut out of plexiglass but can also be 3D printed easily.
